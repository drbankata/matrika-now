Perhaps you've noticed

Something in you is asking to be heard

Not loudly. Not all at once. Just a quiet sense that the way you are living and the way you are made have drifted apart.

  • You have done the reading, the courses, perhaps the retreats — and still find yourself returning to the same patterns.
  • You are the one everyone leans on, yet you rarely feel held yourself.
  • Your life looks fine from the outside, but inside something feels unfinished.
  • You swing between doing too much and feeling too little.
  • You sense there is a more natural way for you to live, work and love — you just can't quite name it yet.

None of this means something is wrong with you. More often, it means something in you is ready to be understood.

When you understand your inner design, life stops feeling random.

Most of us spend years trying to become someone else — calmer, stronger, more disciplined, more like the people around us. The work here is quieter, and far more freeing. It begins by seeing what is already true about you:

Before you begin

Questions people often ask

If you are wondering whether this is for you, you are in good company.

Do I need to know anything about archetypes or Ayurveda?

Not at all. You bring your life, your questions and your curiosity. The language and the frameworks are introduced gently, only as far as they are useful to you.

What if I don't know what I'm looking for?

That is often the most honest place to begin. Many people arrive with a feeling rather than a goal — a restlessness, a heaviness, a sense of being out of step. Naming it is part of the work.

How do I choose between individual coaching, group programmes and online programmes?

Individual coaching suits moments that need depth and privacy. Group programmes suit those who grow through shared experience. Online programmes suit those who want structure they can follow at their own pace. If you are unsure, begin with a conversation.

Is this coaching or therapy?

It is coaching and guided self-inquiry. Malvika works with your patterns, habits and choices in the present; she does not diagnose or treat mental or physical health conditions. If you are receiving therapy or medical care, this work can sit alongside it.

How will the online programmes work?

Each programme is followed online, at your own pace, with short videos, guided audio, a workbook and reflection prompts. Access details, prices and refund terms will be published when enrolment opens; join the waitlist to hear first.

How do the online reflections work?

The archetype and dosha reflections are short sets of questions that you answer in your browser. Your answers are not sent or stored anywhere; the result is simply a starting point for a conversation.

Is this a replacement for therapy or medical care?

No. This work complements professional medical and psychological care; it does not replace it. If you are in crisis or managing a health condition, please continue to work with your doctor or therapist.
