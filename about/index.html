Meet Malvika Varma

A lifelong student of human behaviour, wellbeing and transformation, and the heart of Matrika Now.

In Malvika's words

Wellbeing begins with understanding ourselves

I believe that the journey towards wellbeing begins with understanding ourselves.

Over three decades of professional experience, combined with my own journey of learning and self-discovery, have deepened my interest in the connection between our thoughts, emotions, behaviours and physical wellbeing.

I have completed the Lifestyle and Wellness Coaching programme at Harvard Medical School and a year-long certification programme in Compassionate Inquiry, developed by Dr Gabor Maté. My learning and practice also draw on NLP, hypnotherapy, yoga, meditation, sound healing and other mind-body approaches.

At Matrika Now, I bring these perspectives together to create spaces for reflection, awareness and meaningful change. My work is grounded in a simple belief: we do not always need to become someone different. Sometimes we need the space, awareness and courage to rediscover who we already are.

My approach: evidence-informed wellbeing, compassionate self-inquiry, ancient wisdom, practical change.
